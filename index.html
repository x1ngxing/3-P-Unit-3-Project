OVERVIEW

Patient ███, Alice stumbled into the emergency room disheveled and disoriented towards midnight on October 31st with a bleeding head and apparent trauma to the upper body. Her brother, Rachid, Oliver, had been supporting her the way to Heartspine. Alice, who was too disoriented to coherently communicate, appeared to be too dazed to properly answer questions, with Oliver claiming her to have "fallen down the stairs" and creating her injuries. Throughout the surgery to recover her, she rotated between a coma and certain death multiple times. As the surgery finished, Alice was brought back to consciousness in a new, "undead" state, in old Heartspine limbs, morphine, and prayers sowed the girl back to "life".
